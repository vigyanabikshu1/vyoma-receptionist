## Science Labs

**It is a place where child gains knowledge in different aspects of Science by doing experiments. We have Physics, Chemistry, Biology (Botany & Zoology) Lab. In Biology slides, specimens, charts & experiments based on plants and animals is conducted and through this process more information related to plants and animals is imparted to the minds of student so that they will have knowledge about plants and animals  which are the part of earth.In Physics there are facilities to do experiments on branches of physics like mechanics, light, electricity, sound etc...so that the children will get more information about the concepts by doing experiments. In chemistry lab there are chemicals and equipments to perform  salt analysis, to find the normality of solution and do experiments on organic compounds.**
