## Food And Canteen

**SCRUMPTIOUS AND DELICIOUS FOOD FROM THE OOTUPURA OF BSS GURUKULAM**

**There is a famous saying "Healthy mind lives in a healthy body".Our Management and mess workers are keen and vigilant in providing healthy and nutritious food to the students and teachers.We serve total vegetarian food and the diet includes breakfast,snacks ,lunch ,fruits and vegetables.A wholesome healthy diet is provided in our mess.A total  of 200 students can be accommodated in our mess at a time and almost all our teachers dine with our students which in turn helps them to develop good table manners.**
