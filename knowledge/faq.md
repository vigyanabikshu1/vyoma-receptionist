# About V-Robotics

V-Robotics is a robotics and embedded-systems company that builds
affordable AI-powered robots, including the Vyoma receptionist robot.
The office is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. It is closed
on Sundays and public holidays.

# Office location and directions

The V-Robotics office is on the 2nd floor. The conference room is to the
left of the reception desk, and the lab is at the end of the corridor on
the right. Restrooms are near the lift lobby. Visitor parking is
available in the basement; take the lift to the 2nd floor.

# Meeting staff

Visitors who have an appointment should give their name and whom they
want to meet, and the receptionist will notify that staff member. Walk-in
visitors without an appointment are welcome; the receptionist records
their details and checks whether the staff member is available.

# Careers and internships

V-Robotics offers internships for engineering students in embedded
systems, AI, and robotics. Candidates can email their resume to
careers@v-robotics.example or leave their details with the receptionist.

# Wi-Fi for guests

Guest Wi-Fi is available. Network name: VRobotics-Guest. Ask the
receptionist for the current password after checking in.

# Deliveries

Couriers should leave packages at the reception desk. The receptionist
logs the delivery and notifies the addressee.
