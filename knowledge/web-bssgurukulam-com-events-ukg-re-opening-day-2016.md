## UKG RE-OPENING DAY-2016

#### 06-Jun-2016


First impressions matter – especially on the first day of school. Students arrive with a mix of emotions. Most, however, are nervous. The reasons vary from child to child, but kids tend to feel anxious about starting school. Our teachers made the children feel a safe and warm welcome to the UKG classes.Cute and beautiful decorations were done and masks were given to children to make them feel happy and to rejoice.
