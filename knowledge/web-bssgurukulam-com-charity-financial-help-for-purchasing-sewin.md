## Financial Help for purchasing sewing machine

#### 10-Mar-2020


‘HOPE’ became the pillar for Ms. Thansiya to support her family for future life by giving sewing machine worth Rs. 12190. She is the only person who is supporting her family as her mother is paralyzed and father is no more.
