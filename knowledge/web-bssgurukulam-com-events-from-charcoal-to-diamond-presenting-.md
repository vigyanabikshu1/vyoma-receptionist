## "From Charcoal to Diamond" Presenting Proud Alumnies

#### 07-Jul-2023


A motivational program called Charcoal to Diamond was organized under the leadership of two former students who have struggled to catch up with others in their studies.

The inspiration imparted by Fatah Kaja Moideen, who is now conquering the skies as a pilot after being a little behind during her studies, and violinist Anjali Salin, who is a grad artist from Trinity College, London, will surely be a great light for our children.
