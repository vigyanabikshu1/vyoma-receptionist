#### 21-Jan-2024


Ms. Maria, Chairperson of Ramanunni Project, Ms. Marta trustee in Spain, Ramanunni project, along with their relative Rema Radhakrishnan visited our school premises as a part of Collaborative project for 9 th grade children with Sant Ignasi, Barcelona.
