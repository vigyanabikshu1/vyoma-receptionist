## Celebrating Reading Week

#### 23-Jun-2023


Famous writer Sri. T D Ramakrishnan (Kerala Sahithya Academy Winner) visited our holy land marking the significance of reading. Sri Ajesh was honoured on this day as he has been a pioneer in distributing books to children of various rural areas. He has a moving Library Box in which books are collected for this purpose.
