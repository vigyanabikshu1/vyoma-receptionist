## 2016 World Environment Day

#### 06-Jun-2016


We enthusiastically with great excitement celebrated "WORLD ENVIRONMENT DAY" on June 6th Monday in our school. School leader Vipin Suresh took an oath related to environment, which was repeated by all the students of our school. Hridya Vasudevan delivered a speech on the topic "Environment Protection". Few students exhibited placards in all the classes related to the importance of protecting the environment. Clear messages were spread throughout the campus with the help of charts and pictures related to pollution and environment. We showed videos related to environment day in the classes.
