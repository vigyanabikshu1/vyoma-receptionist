## Honoured Solitary Cycle Rider Mr. Faiz Ashraf

#### 24-Aug-2022


Faiz Ashraf Ali who triumphs a 'Kerala to London solitary cycle ride' to spread "Harmony beyond boundaries" takes the energy of Gurukulam and proceeds to London for a 30000 km ride crossing 35 countries.
