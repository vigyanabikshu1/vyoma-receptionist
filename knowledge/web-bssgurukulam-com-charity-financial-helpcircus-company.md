## Financial Help-Circus Company

#### 12-May-2020


**HOPE has once again lend it’s hand to Apollo Circus Company, now at Bangalore. Held up with lockdown, consisting of above 55 members who are in misery in all sense.**

**We could support them by offering the sum of Rs. 50000. (Information through Asianet News 10th May 2020) **

**For the second time on june 23rd  2020, HOPE added colour to the Apollo Circus Company by providing Rs. 10000 and being a support to their organization.**
