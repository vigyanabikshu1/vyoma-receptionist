## Grand Parent's Day Celebration

#### 14-Nov-2015


BSS Gurukulam celebrated the auspicious Grandparents and Children’s Day celebration on 14th November 2015.

Distinguished MLA of Alathur , Shri M.Chandran inaugurated the function and shared his words of wisdom with us. Two respected and esteemed guests Dr.Jinaraj Das DCH and Dr.Sunandha DGO were the Chief Guests of this glorious event. This day of celebration was colorful with the various performances of the dazzling kiddies of Gurukulam.
