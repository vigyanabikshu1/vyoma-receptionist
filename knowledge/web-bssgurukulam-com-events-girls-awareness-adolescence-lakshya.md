## GIRLS AWARENESS-ADOLESCENCE-LAKSHYA

#### 15-Jun-2016



An Awareness Programme was conducted for the girls of VIII STD and XI on the topic Adolescence.The class was headed by Meena Tr and Priya Tr to make the girls understand about the problems faced by girls during the adolescence period which they are not able to disclose to their parents or elders.An inspiring and heart touching video 'Yathra' was shown to them.This programme aimed at increasing the awareness of girls in areas of active living,positive self regard and resiliency.
