## Healthy Children Wealthy Gurukulam

#### 27-Jun-2016


Fruits made up of charts were prepared and students were taken to all the classes to ensure the importance of fruits in their day to day life.

Dr.Lakshmi came to take class for 7th section on the Doctor's day on the topic 'Healthy food habits'. 

To make the students aware of the importance of vegetables Aparna P of UKG A delivered a speech on 'Recipe for making vegetable salad'.
