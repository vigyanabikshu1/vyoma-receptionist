## CHARITY -Team Upasana with HOPE members

#### 07-Jun-2016



"Helping a person will not change the world but it will change the world for that person."

The Upasana Team teachers along with HOPE members and Vanitha College students visited GLP School Venganoor ,Pudhiyankam L.P School and Perinkulam L.P School to donate Books for financially challenged students.
