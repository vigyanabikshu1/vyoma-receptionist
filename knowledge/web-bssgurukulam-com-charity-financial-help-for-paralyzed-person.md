## Financial help for Paralyzed person

#### 08-Jul-2020


Mr. Chandranpilla from Thenkurussi, who is paralyzed and under treatment was given an amount of Rs. 5000 towards further treatment. The amount was handed over to his wife, Mrs. Baby.
