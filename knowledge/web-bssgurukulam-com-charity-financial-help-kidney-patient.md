## Financial Help - Kidney Patient

#### 29-Oct-2020


Mr. Prabhakumar Nair, who was surviving from kidney failure got assisted through a monetary sum of Rs. 10000 and the money was handed over to his brother Mr. Sakthidharan Nair.
