## Gandhi Jayathi 2023

#### 02-Oct-2023


The 154th birth anniversary of the Father of the Nation was duly celebrated by paying floral tributes to the statue of Gandhiji and sharing memories of the great soul. The ceremony became a striking reminder to follow Gandhian ideals.
