## NEW TEACHER'S MEETING (KALOTSAVAM)

#### 14-Jun-2016


The Lakshya Team united all the new teachers in the B.Ed Hall and showed them the Annual Report of 2015-2016 to bring up their keen interest in the areas of of Arts and Sports. This meeting was addressed by B.Ed College Principal Mrs. Balambika Madam & Kalotsavam Head Mrs.Radhika Teacher. . We team felt proud to have this session where we could share our experiences in Arts, Fairs & Sports which gave a spark in the minds of new teachers.
