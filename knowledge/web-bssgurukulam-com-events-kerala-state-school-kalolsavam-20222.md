## Kerala State School Kalolsavam 2022-23

#### 07-Jan-2023


Asia's Biggest Cultural Event 2022-23

As we were into the Golden Jubilee year, we were fortunate to taste the 10th year Champions title at  the Asia's Cultural Art fest - Kalothsavam. 

The event witnessed the glory of BSS which hailed the title for the 10th year. In history, now it's the time to encrypt the lines that--

'It's Gurukulam, that's crowned as Champions for Decades'

Really, it was a Teamwork, topped with coordination, compassion, compromises and confidence.

We feel extremely delighted with this victory, which is engrossed with the prestigious autographs of our prime leader, Sir. Vijayan. V.Anand, our gems - the students, the Core teachers, in charge Teachers and Staffs.

We again proved that 'its the hard work and passion that counts'.
