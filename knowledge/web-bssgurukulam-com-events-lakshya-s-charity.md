## Lakshya's charity

#### 06-Jul-2016


Charity - The sceptre of Gurukulam

We, Lakshya team collected old newspapers, magazines & notebooks from students of Ist Std to XII std and got an amount of Rs. 3000/- which we handed over to HOPE organisation. With the whole hearted support of Hope members, we the team members provided lunch to Autism students (50 Nos) of Jeevandhara Charitable Trust - Perinkulam & Swathi Junction. We were also supported by B.Ed Trainees and teachers. It was really a heart touching programme where we could see each autism student having different talents. Seeing them tears shed from eyes making us feel how lucky each of us are to be in this holy land and thanked almighty. Prayed for their future.
