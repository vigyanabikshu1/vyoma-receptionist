## A Token of Love for Palakkad Scouts and Guides Training Centre

#### 24-Aug-2024


Alathur BSS Gurukulam Higher Secondary School Management sponsored 2 tents to Kakkayur Scouts and Guides Training Center.

Principal Dr. Vijayan V Anand handed over the check to Palakkad District Association.
