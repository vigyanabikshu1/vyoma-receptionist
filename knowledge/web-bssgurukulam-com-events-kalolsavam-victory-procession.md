## Kalolsavam Victory Procession

#### 13-Jan-2025


Palakkad District Police Chief Sri. Ajith Kumar IPS inaugurated and addressed the Kalolsavam Victory Procession. Eminent writer Sri. Mundur Sethumadhavan and other distinguished representatives of reputed organizations felicitated the procession at Palakkad town. Earlier the victorious team of students and teachers received warm appreciations from Alathur and Vadakkencherry. Honble MLA Sri. KD Prasenan and representatives of Alathur Panchayath lead the ceremony at Alathur. Former Minister Sri. KE Ismail and representatives of 'Voice of Vadakkencherry' welcomed the team with mementos sweets.
