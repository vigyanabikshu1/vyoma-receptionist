📚 Open-Book Exam (RAG)

  • Our website & documents become its "textbook"
  • Each question fetches the 3 most relevant facts
  • Vyoma answers only from those facts — no making things up!

    • 🛠️ Agentic = Takes Action

  • Registers the visitor's name & company
  • Logs every visit with purpose & time
  • Sends "your guest is waiting" alerts to staff

    • 🌏 Why Offline Matters

  • Privacy: voices & faces never leave this laptop
  • Cost: no subscriptions, no cloud API fees
  • Reliability: works even where internet doesn't
